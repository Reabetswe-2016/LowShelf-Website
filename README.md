## Project Overview

This project involves the development of a professional multi-page website for Lowveld Shelving and Racking (LowShelf).

LowShelf is a South African family-owned company based in White River, Mpumalanga. The company has more than 30 years of experience in shopfitting and focuses on industrial and warehouse shelving and racking solutions.

The website is being developed to provide customers with accessible information about LowShelf, its products and services, completed projects and ways to request a quotation or make an enquiry.

---

## Website Goals and Objectives

The main goal of the website is to create a professional and user-friendly online platform for LowShelf.

The objectives are to:

- Provide information about LowShelf.
- Present products and services.
- Display product and project images.
- Provide information about shelving and racking solutions.
- Allow users to request a quotation.
- Provide contact information.
- Create a professional online presence.

to create a responsive layout.
