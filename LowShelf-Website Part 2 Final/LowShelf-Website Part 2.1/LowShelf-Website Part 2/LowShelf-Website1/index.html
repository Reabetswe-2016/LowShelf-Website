Quality Shelving and Racking Solutions

Lowveld Shelving and Racking is a South African family-owned company based in White River, Mpumalanga. With more than 30 years of experience in shopfitting, the company focuses on providing industrial and warehouse shelving and racking solutions.

LowShelf provides storage solutions that are suitable for warehouses, factories, workshops, garages and other industrial environments. Its shelving and racking systems can be customised to suit different spaces, products and storage requirements.
